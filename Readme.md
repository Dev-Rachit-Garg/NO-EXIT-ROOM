# 👁️ No Escape Room

**A haunted storytelling experience that turns into a book-selling website.**

What starts as a mysterious story gradually pulls you deeper into the unknown. All you have to do is press **Enter** and keep going. But as the story unfolds, you begin to discover that there's more to it than meets the eye.

And just when you think you've reached the end, the real twist begins.

##  About the Project

**No Escape Room** is a horror-themed, interactive e-commerce website designed to sell a book through an immersive storytelling experience.

Instead of presenting visitors with a traditional product page, the website builds suspense from the very first interaction. Users progress through the experience by repeatedly pressing **Enter**, uncovering fragments of a mysterious story before reaching the final reveal — the book itself.

The story they experience at the beginning becomes the hook that connects them to the book being sold at the end.

The goal is simple: **make visitors experience the story before asking them to buy it.**

##  How It Works

1. **Enter the Unknown** — Visitors land on a dark, mysterious opening screen.
2. **Follow the Story** — Pressing Enter advances the narrative, revealing the story piece by piece.
3. **Build the Suspense** — The experience gradually creates curiosity and a sense of unease.
4. **Discover the Twist** — The narrative leads to a reveal that connects the experience to the book.
5. **Enter the Store** — The final page shifts the experience toward the book's product and purchase information.

The result is a blend of interactive fiction, a lightweight browser game, and an e-commerce landing page.

##  What Makes It Different?

Most book-selling websites introduce the product first and try to convince visitors to buy it.

**No Escape Room takes a different approach.**

- **Story-first marketing:** Engage visitors before introducing the product.
- **Minimal interaction:** A simple Enter-to-continue mechanic keeps the experience easy to follow.
- **Horror atmosphere:** The haunted theme supports the narrative and builds suspense.
- **A narrative-driven reveal:** The story and the product page are connected rather than feeling like separate experiences.
- **Game-like browsing:** Exploring the website feels closer to progressing through a short horror game than browsing a conventional online store.

## 🎯 The Idea Behind It

The interesting part of this project isn't just selling a horror book. It's experimenting with **how a website can use curiosity and storytelling to make a product more engaging.**

Rather than treating an online store as a collection of product listings, No Escape Room treats the entire browsing experience as part of the story.

Built in approximately 2.7 hours as a beginner project, it represents an exploration of interactive storytelling and creative web development.
